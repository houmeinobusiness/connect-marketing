01 / THE PROBLEM

Where Japan entries
usually stall

The site gets built. Then it stops
working as a business tool.

A translation, not a sales tool

The site is your home-country page in Japanese words. The trust signals, the wording, and the inquiry path don’t match how Japanese customers actually decide.

Ads and pages that don’t line up

Campaigns send traffic, but the landing page doesn’t answer the ad’s promise — in Japanese, for this market. The clicks are paid for; the inquiries don’t follow.

Nobody owns what happens after launch

Inquiries, follow-ups, and improvements fall between headquarters and local vendors, and the site quietly stops producing.

Hiring gets the same treatment

Recruiting pages are published once and left alone — even though hiring in Japan needs its own local approach, just like acquisition.

The common thread: launch is treated as the finish line.

Connect Marketing is built the other way. A page starts earning its place after it goes live — when someone keeps running and improving it.

05 / FAQ

Questions, answered

Q1

Do you work in English?

Yes — and everything is handled by email. Consultations, proposals, progress reports, and deliverables are shared in writing, in English; no phone or video calls are needed at any stage. The customer-facing work — websites, copy, and campaigns for your Japanese audience — is produced in Japanese.

Q2

We’re not based in Japan. Can we still work together?

Yes. The process is fully asynchronous and email-based, which works naturally across time zones. I’m based in Tokyo (JST); you never need to match my working hours to keep a project moving.

Q3

We already have a Japanese website, but it isn’t producing inquiries. Can you help?

Yes. A standard way to start is a review of the existing site: the inquiry path, the messaging, and the measurement setup. Then we fix what matters most, in order.

Q4

Do you handle recruiting pages as well?

Yes. Hiring follows the same principle as acquisition: a page planned from the goal, then improved after launch based on how it performs.

Q5

How does a project start?

With an email to t.chida@connect-mkt.jp. Tell me where you are with Japan and what you want the page to achieve. You’ll receive a written proposal with scope and an estimate before anything is built — no obligation to proceed.
